HAND TRACKING OFFLINE

Pick a tool, then pinch and drag in the air (or left-drag with the mouse). Pen and Wire follow your hand in depth too. After each shape the tool returns to Move so you can grab things again.

TOOL: MOVE

SESSION

Solo mode. Host or join a room to share the model.
Pinch over a shape: grab and move. Pull your hand toward the camera to bring it closer, push away to send it back.
Fist: move your hand to rotate the selected shape.
Two-hand pinch: spread to scale, twist to roll.
Open palm: move it away from the dashed ring to orbit the camera. Two open palms: spread to zoom, move together to pan.
Mouse: click selects, right-drag orbits, wheel zooms. Keys: [ further, ] nearer, Del removes.
